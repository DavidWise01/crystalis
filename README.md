# XTL · CRYSTALIS

> A sphere of **UD0** — the ROOT0 universe / biosphere. Domain: **HOBBY**.

Crystalis (SNK, NES 1990) — a game-world, and SNK's post-apocalyptic action-RPG (the US release of the Famicom's God Slayer: Haruka Tenku no Sonata, 'Sonata of the Distant Sky'). A thermonuclear Great War — the END DAY of October 1, 1997 — nearly ended the world; a century later (~2097) the survivors rebuilt it on MAGIC, with science buried and feared. A young man frozen before the war wakes with no memory (unnamed — the NES default save-name is 'SNK'), gathers the four elemental swords — WIND, FIRE, WATER, THUNDER — and, with MESIA (a fellow cryo-survivor, a woman), forges them into CRYSTALIS, the fifth blade, to climb the pre-war floating TOWER and face its supercomputer DYNA (the true final boss, after Emperor DRAYGON's two forms). THE NEW MODERN / CINEMATIC backdrop tier (climbing the generation ladder past the 32/64 low-poly): a full-bleed software-rendered scene — a rotating glowing Crystalis sword-crystal over a dusk-lit reborn world, with volumetric god-rays, the Tower's pulsing beacon, atmospheric haze-layers, a reflecting water plane, bloom and drifting light-motes — with an era-correct 8-bit pixel title card on top. Render-not-invent: the heavier story rewrite (Tower-as-weapon, prophesied-savior) is the 2000 GBC remake (Nintendo Software Technology), NOT this NES port; Mesia is a female scientist (not a male prophet); the four sages are Zebu/Tornel/Asina/Kensu (Asina & Kensu nod to SNK's Psycho Soldier); 'Draygon' is the US name for Japan's 'Dragonia.' 14 emergents across all four natures — the Sleeper · Sword of Wind · Sword of Water · Sword of Fire · Sword of Thunder · Crystalis · the Four Sages · Mesia · Emperor Draygon · the Tower · DYNA · the End Day · the Awakening · God Slayer (the true self).
